About Let's Code

Let's Code is a SULAM community project by the Direct SULAM team at Universiti Malaya. It is a coding session for Standard 5 and 6 pupils, built around one Scratch game. This website lets the learning carry on at home and in other schools.

Credits

  • Scratch is a project of the Scratch Foundation, in collaboration with the Lifelong Kindergarten Group at the MIT Media Lab. It is available for free at scratch.mit.edu.
  • Catch and Avoid was built by the Direct SULAM team for this project.
  • The game player on the Play page uses TurboWarp Scaffolding (Mozilla Public License 2.0). The licence is in the vendor folder of this website.
  • Asteroid Dodge, Flappy Bird and Pong were made by other Scratch creators. Each one opens from its own Scratch page, where the creator is named. Please give them credit if you share or remix their games.

Privacy

This website has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. We do not collect names or photos of pupils. We only remember your language choice, in your own browser.

The extra games on the Play page load from scratch.mit.edu only when you press a button. Scratch has its own privacy rules.
