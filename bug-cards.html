Bug Cards

A bug is a mistake in code. Each card shows a game that is broken. Your job is to Watch what goes wrong, Try to find the mistake, and Fix it.

How to use a card

  1. Save a copy of your game first (File, then Save to your computer).
  2. Break your game on purpose in the same way as the card.
  3. Watch what happens in the game.
  4. Answer the Try question, find the mistake and fix it.
